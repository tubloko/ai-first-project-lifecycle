# Project Atlas

## Objective

Publish a concise onboarding guide that lets a new reader understand the project’s purpose, choose a relevant path, and begin without reading the full documentation set.

## Current-state entry point

Read [CURRENT_STATE.md](CURRENT_STATE.md), then follow the active task link.

## Canonical homes

| Information | Path | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | `PROJECT.md` | Human owner |
| Current position | `CURRENT_STATE.md` | Decision layer |
| Task contracts | `tasks/` | Brain |
| Decisions | `decisions/` | Named decision authority |
| Human approvals | `approvals/APPROVALS.md` | Human owner |
| Task evidence | `evidence/` | Executor and Reviewer |
| Research knowledge and reports | `roles/research/` | Research role |

## Work levels

| Workstream | Level | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Routine corrections | Level 1 | Reversible and directly inspectable |
| Research notes | Level 2 | Must survive multiple sessions |
| Landing-page structure | Level 3 | Requires a domain report, clean execution, review, and approval |

## Human-only decisions

- final public promise;
- subjective visual composition;
- publication;
- accepting unresolved high-impact findings.

## Archive rule

Superseded decisions remain in `decisions/` with `SUPERSEDED` status and a link to their replacement. They are not deleted or treated as current.
