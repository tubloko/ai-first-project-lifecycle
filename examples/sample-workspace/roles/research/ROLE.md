# Research role

## Mandate

Identify evidence-backed onboarding patterns and apply them to bounded project questions.

## Owned knowledge

Reusable research notes and their provenance live in `knowledge_base/`.

## Outputs

Dated reports in `reports/` that answer one project question against a named project state.

## Authority

- Observe: yes.
- Research: yes.
- Recommend: yes.
- Decide project structure: no.
- Approve public output: no.
- Publish: no.

## Stop conditions

- sources materially disagree;
- a recommendation depends on an unresolved project promise;
- available evidence cannot support a conclusion;
- the question crosses another role’s authority.
