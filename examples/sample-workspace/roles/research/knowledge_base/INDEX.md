# Research knowledge base

## Scope

Reusable evidence about helping new readers understand and enter a documented project.

## Source policy

- Prefer primary research and first-party documentation.
- Record access dates for changing sources.
- Label project-specific inference explicitly.
- Do not promote a report conclusion into reusable knowledge without reviewing its evidence.
