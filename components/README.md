# Components

Components are canonical building blocks shared by the Levels and specialized pipelines.

- [Memory and context](memory-and-context.md) — canonical homes, routing, freshness, and archives.
- [Model and context routing](model-and-context-routing.md) — capability, effort, context, and escalation by responsibility.
- [Role architecture](role-architecture.md) — mandates, knowledge bases, reports, and role boundaries.
- [Authority and approvals](authority-and-approvals.md) — permission verbs, human-only decisions, and fail-closed gates.
- [Evidence and verification](evidence-and-verification.md) — claim-to-evidence mapping, verifier trust, and honest accounting.
- [Review and triage](review-and-triage.md) — distinct review lenses, inbox behavior, and dispositions.
- [Integration and landing](integration-and-landing.md) — work lines, atomic units, revert units, and the landing gate.
- [Workflow evolution](workflow-evolution.md) — process proposals, controlled experiments, and rule retirement.

A Level composes these components. Do not copy a component into several Level folders and edit the copies independently.
