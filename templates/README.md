# Template catalog

Templates are starting shapes. Remove sections that do not protect a real boundary, and add project-specific fields only when their owner and use are clear.

## Foundation

- [Project instructions](project-instructions.md) — objective, canonical homes, selected Levels, and global boundaries.
- [Current state](current-state.md) — active position and next-session routing.
- [Decision record](decision-record.md) — options, evidence, authority, and consequences.
- [Approval log](approval-log.md) — append-only human decisions tied to exact artifacts.
- [Model routing](model-routing.md) — capability, effort, context, verification, and escalation by responsibility.

## Work

- [Task brief](task-brief.md) — Level 1 and lightweight Level 2 work.
- [Task contract](task-spec.md) — resolved Level 3 or Level 4 execution contract.
- [Evidence bundle](evidence-bundle.md) — claims, artifacts, verification, review, and residual risk.
- [Review finding](review-finding.md) — one inspectable issue and its disposition.
- [Review inbox](review-inbox.md) — unresolved findings only.

## Roles and research

- [Role contract](role-contract.md) — mandate, inputs, outputs, knowledge, and authority.
- [Knowledge-base index](knowledge-base-index.md) — routing and freshness for reusable domain knowledge.
- [Research brief](research-brief.md) — bounded external research question.
- [Research report](research-report.md) — sourced findings and project implication.
- [Domain report](domain-report.md) — a persistent role’s verdict or recommendation.

## Visual work

- [Visual brief](visual-brief.md) — purpose, composition, constraints, provenance, and approval.
- [Asset index](asset-index.md) — candidate, approved, rejected, and retired visual artifacts.
- [Visual verification report](visual-verification-report.md) — reference, capture, comparison, semantic checks, and human judgment.

## Autonomy and evolution

- [Run contract](run-contract.md) — eligible work, budget, routing, authority, and stop conditions.
- [Run report](run-report.md) — integrated, blocked, and unresolved run outcomes.
- [Workflow experiment](workflow-experiment.md) — pre-registered process hypothesis and decision criterion.
